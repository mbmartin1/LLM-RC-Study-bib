**Why it is here:** cited in the introduction for the claim that LLM capability is improving rapidly and that models are now strong at coding and reasoning. It motivates the premise that there are meaningful capability tiers to compare (e.g. Opus 3 vs Opus 5).

**Caveat:** this is trade press covering METR's task-length-horizon work, not a peer-reviewed source. It is fine as a motivating citation but should not carry an empirical claim in the argument.

**Recommended action:** replace or supplement with the underlying METR paper on time horizons of model task completion, and cite this only as popular coverage. A reviewer will notice a magazine citation doing load-bearing work in the first paragraph of a thesis proposal.

**Status:** background — the weakest citation in the current reference list, flagged for upgrade.
