**Why it is here:** this is the theoretical anchor of the proposal. It is the review that states the claim the whole study is built to test — that language structure is shaped by pressure to minimize the processing cost of communicative success.

**How it is used:** cited in the opening sentence as the umbrella statement of the efficiency account. Dependency length minimization ([[futrell-2015-dependency-length]]) and uniform information density ([[jaeger-2010-redundancy-and-reduction]]) are the two specific instantiations we take from it and operationalize as measures.

**The gap it leaves open:** the causal claim in this framework is hard to test, because every natural language has always had listeners, so there is no comparison case where comprehender pressure is absent. That gap is the opening the LLM natural-experiment design is designed to fill.
