**Why it is here:** establishes the premise the study depends on — that LLM text differs from human text in measurable grammatical and rhetorical ways, not just in vibe. Without this, the descriptive half of the design has no prior reason to expect an effect.

**How it is used:** cited as the demonstration that human/AI divergence is measurable at the level of linguistic features. It also gives a candidate feature inventory to compare against our own efficiency measures.

**Where our study goes further:** this paper characterizes the divergence but stops there. It does not ask whether human readers *pay* for that divergence in processing cost, and it does not vary model capability systematically. Those two moves are the contribution of the thesis, so this is the paper the introduction should pivot off of.

**To do:** check whether their feature set overlaps enough with dependency length and surprisal variance that we can report our measures alongside theirs for continuity.
