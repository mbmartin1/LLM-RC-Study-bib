**Why it is here:** the source of SUBTLEX-US, the word frequency norms the descriptive analysis uses for log word frequency over content words.

**How it is used:** purely instrumental — it is the citation for the measure, and the justification for choosing subtitle-based norms over Kučera–Francis. The argument that matters for us is that SUBTLEX frequencies predict lexical decision and reading times better than the older norms, which is exactly the criterion we care about since our dependent variable is reading time.

**Practical note:** SUBTLEX-US is a fixed 2009 corpus, so recently coined and technical vocabulary will be missing or badly estimated. LLM outputs may use more of that vocabulary than student writing does, which would show up as spurious low frequency. Decide how to handle out-of-vocabulary items before running the analysis, and report the OOV rate per condition.

**Status:** method citation, not a theoretical source.
